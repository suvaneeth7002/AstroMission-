Data sources. Planet mass, diameter, gravity, escape velocity and distance from the Sun: NASA Planetary Fact Sheet (NSSDCA); Mars values cross-checked on the Mars Fact Sheet. Transfer orbits use the standard Hohmann transfer and the rocket equation with these values.

Game balance. Costs, instrument masses, rocket sizes and science points are simplified for play and are not real mission figures.
